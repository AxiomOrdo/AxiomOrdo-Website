# AO-PDF Acceptable Use Policy

This operational-draft policy applies to AO-PDF, provided by AxiomOrdo Ltd. Contact hello@axiomordo.com with questions.

## Permitted use

Use AO-PDF only for documents you are legally authorised to process and only for lawful purposes.

## Prohibited use

Do not use AO-PDF to infringe intellectual-property, privacy or confidentiality rights; facilitate fraud, harassment or unlawful discrimination; distribute malware; defeat document security; misrepresent altered records as authentic; or process illegal content.

Do not attempt to bypass input limits, exploit the browser worker, interfere with service availability, or use automated traffic in a way that harms other users or AxiomOrdo infrastructure.

## Sensitive material

Although admitted workflows are browser-local, you remain responsible for device security, browser extensions, downloads and local storage. Review your environment before processing sensitive or regulated material.
