What’s inside

Seven sections and a final ten-point walk-round. Built for maritime QHSE practice, not generic office safety.

Certificates and LSA

The documents inspectors open with, plus lifeboats, liferafts, HRUs, pyrotechnics, muster lists and drill evidence.

Fire safety and MARPOL

Fire doors, dampers, fixed systems, escape routes, Oil Record Book, OWS, garbage, sewage, fuel and ballast records.

MLC and navigation

Hours of rest, SEAs, welfare records, medicals, corrected charts, bridge equipment, passage plans and GMDSS.

ISM and final walk-round

SMS access, maintenance evidence, NCR close-out, familiarisation, DPA details and the ten things people miss.
