Who should apply

The pilot is designed for professionals able to judge whether the pack would work in a real maritime management-system environment.

QHSE and compliance leaders

QHSE managers, compliance managers, DPAs and marine assurance professionals.

Operational managers

Fleet, technical and marine superintendents with responsibility for controlled processes and evidence.

Experienced consultants

Independent maritime auditors and consultants with direct experience of management systems and implementation.

Relevant practical experience

Participants should be able to assess usability, governance, implementation barriers and likely management value.

What participation involves

The purpose is to understand the full learning journey, including whether early uncertainty resolves as the pieces come together.

Work through the pack

Review each stage as though you were responsible for applying it within a maritime organisation.

Record weekly experience

Provide brief feedback on clarity, effort, relevance, implementation barriers and what became clearer that week.

Complete the final evaluation

Revisit earlier concerns and distinguish genuine defects from onboarding issues or an expected learning curve.

Choose the safest review route

Use a hypothetical scenario, a familiar past case or a limited real example where your organisation permits it.

No board or shareholder approval is required for a desktop dry run. You do not need to deploy AI, change controlled documents, disclose confidential information, monitor employees or commit to a purchase.

Live implementation is optional, not a condition of participation.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need formal company approval?

Not for a personal professional review or desktop simulation, subject to your own employment and confidentiality obligations. Any live implementation must follow your organisation's normal approval process.

Must my organisation already use AI?

No. The framework can be reviewed using a hypothetical or retrospective scenario.

How much time is required?

You should be able to work through the assigned material each week and provide concise structured feedback. Exact effort will vary and is itself part of what the pilot is testing.

Can independent consultants apply?

Yes, where they have sufficient maritime QHSE, compliance, audit or management-system experience.

Can the editable files be changed later?

They may be adapted for authorised internal use under the final licence terms. Redistribution and commercial reuse are prohibited.

What happens if I do not complete the feedback?

The permanent internal-use licence is conditional on completing the agreed pilot feedback process.
