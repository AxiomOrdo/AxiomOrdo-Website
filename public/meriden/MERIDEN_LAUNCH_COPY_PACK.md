## Nurture email 3 — DPA visibility

Subject: The DPA cannot fix what the vessel never surfaces

One reason PSC readiness fails is simple: the office does not see the same vessel the inspector sees.

The DPA may have the records. The superintendent may have the dashboard. But the PSCO sees the fire door, the actual escape route, the actual certificate file, the actual crew understanding.

That is why the checklist is useful as a bridge between vessel and office.

If you are on board, use it and send the gaps up early.

## Nurture email 4 — AI boundary

Subject: Where AI helps PSC readiness, and where it must not decide

AI can help with PSC readiness, but only inside the right boundary.

Useful:

- drafting mock inspection questions
- structuring gap lists
- turning raw findings into corrective-action logs
- checking whether evidence has been attached
- summarising vessel readiness status for review

Not acceptable:

- deciding the vessel is compliant
- replacing competent review
- inventing regulatory references
- making safety-critical decisions without a human gate

The practical rule is simple: AI drafts and structures. A competent person decides.

That is the same boundary used in the PSC Inspection AI Readiness Kit.
